## 2026-03-05 - Semantic Tab Controls and Keyboard Access in Explainer Dashboards
**Learning:** High-fidelity interactive explainer frames embedded in slides often overlook core keyboard navigation and screen reader support. Custom-styled tabs without roles and expand/collapse `div` triggers without `tabindex` or keyboard listeners present total barriers to users with assistive technologies. Standardizing tabs with `role="tab"`, `aria-selected`, and `aria-controls` plus equipping custom cards with `tabindex="0"`, `role="button"`, and Enter/Space handlers provides a seamless, accessible user experience.
**Action:** When auditing presentation slides or interactive embedded explainer frameworks, prioritize making all interactive panels (like tab panels, toggle cards, and collapsible segments) keyboard navigable, styled with `:focus-visible`, and labeled with proper ARIA attributes (`aria-expanded`, `aria-selected`).
